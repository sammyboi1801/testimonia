What is inside

Texts
The Leningrad Codex and the Greek New Testament, with the Septuagint, the Vulgate and English translations from Tyndale and the King James Version to the World English Bible, verse-aligned.
Words
An interlinear for every Hebrew and Greek word, with lexicons (BDB, Abbott-Smith, LSJ), grammar and every other occurrence.
Variants
Around 15,000 places where the printed editions or the Hebrew tradition differ, each explained in plain words.
Early writers
Church fathers and Jewish and Roman historians, dated and placed on a timeline, with their quotations of Scripture linked to each verse.
Study aids
Cross references, the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, Old Testament quotations in the New, book introductions and outlines.
Your notes
Highlights and notes kept in your own browser, with export. An optional assistant can search the library using an AI key of your own, or a model on your computer.
