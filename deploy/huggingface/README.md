# Testimonia

An open-source Bible study library: 13 translations with their footnotes, the Greek and Hebrew text word by
word, where the manuscripts differ, the church fathers and Josephus linked to the verses they quote, a
timeline of the early church, and a bring-your-own-key study assistant.
